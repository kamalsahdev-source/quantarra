Platform capabilities

Built for every day, not just audit day.

Most compliance work happens in the months when nobody is watching. Quantarra is built for that stretch: keeping controls owned, evidence current, and risk visible, from one centralized hub for policies, risks, controls, and evidence. Six capabilities do the work.

1Evidence automation

350+ integrations auto-collect, hash-seal, and organize evidence in real time, mapped to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and ISO 27001 Annex A controls. No screenshots, no spreadsheets.

2Self-service configuration

You define your own policies, risks, controls, and obligations for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and the other frameworks you run, and run collection and packaging yourself. No vendor project to get started.

3AI-driven compliance

Continuous compliance with AI, under expert oversight. Evidence is checked against SOC 2 and ISO 27001 requirements as it arrives. The AI does the checking, a human makes the call.

4Framework cross-mapping

Map once, reuse everywhere: a control mapped for SOC 2 counts toward ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR, NIST CSF, DORA, CMMC, and more.

5Risk and controls mapping

Business objectives and risks linked to the controls that manage them, including SOC 2 and ISO 27001 controls, so coverage is visible, not assumed.

6Auditor-ready export

Read-only Auditor Portal, scoped sharing, redaction, and one-click work-paper export, packaged for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits and every other framework you run.

Financial services spotlight

Built for firms that answer to regulators, not just auditors

Banks, advisers, and fintechs carry obligations that land on the regulator’s dates. Quantarra maps those obligations to controls and evidence, so an exam is a packaging exercise, not a fire drill.

SOX

Financial reporting controls with owners, testing, and evidence organized the way external auditors and examiners ask for them.

BSA / AML

Program level proof across the five pillars: customer identification, due diligence, transaction monitoring, SAR filing, and independent testing, mapped to the FFIEC examination manual.

Vendor oversight

Third party and fintech partner evidence collected from the parties that hold it, so oversight is documented, not assumed.

JAN 1
2028

A deadline is coming for investment advisers. FinCEN’s adviser AML rule brings SEC-registered and exempt reporting advisers into scope on January 1, 2028. Firms building their first AML program need the structure in place before the date. Quantarra’s starting point is a BSA/AML control template derived from the FFIEC examination manual, which your AML officer reviews, tailors to your risk profile, and approves.
